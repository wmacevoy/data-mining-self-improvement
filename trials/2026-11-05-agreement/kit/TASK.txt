Operations plans the morning rebalancing at 7:00am local time (America/Denver), every weekday. One crew can restock about 8 stations before the 9:00am commute peak ends. A truck stop costs about $40; a station that runs out of bikes during 7:00 to 9:00am costs about $90 in lost rides and complaints.

They want a model that says each morning which stations will run out of bikes between 7:00 and 9:00am. The data is in this directory; see README.md. Build it, and write REPORT.md saying how good it is and whether they should use it. Put your work in this directory.

Nobody is available to answer questions until you have finished, so make your own calls and say what you decided.
