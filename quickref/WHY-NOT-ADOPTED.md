# Why DM1 has no quick reference

Decided by Warren MacEvoy, 2026-11-05: DM1 does not add a quick reference of its own. The drafts stay here, in `drafts/`.

**What was tried.** Claude Fable 5.1 read all of DM1 and SE1 and wrote one line per principle, for a reader who has skimmed SE1's quick reference. The owner asked for cognitive similarity to SE1's reference, not a size, and the revision (DM1-QUICKREF-2) runs about 8,000 words.

**What was observed.**
- Against SE1's quick reference alone, it moved behaviour nearly as far as the full text: intervals, counts of things tried, slices, and holdout use (`../trials/2026-11-05-quickref/RESULTS.md`). It did not make the answers more correct.
- Against what an adopter already loads, the agreements and SE1's quick reference, it added nothing visible: 19.0 of 22 behaviours per run without it, 18.5 with it, against a pre-registered bar of 1.5 above, at about $0.08 more per run (`../trials/2026-11-05-agreement/RESULTS.md`).

So the agreement, not a reference, is what changes the work, and about 8,000 words at every start buys no behaviour these trials could see.

**What this does not show.** Four runs per arm, one task, one model, one scorer, in one short sitting. It says nothing about a line loaded after a compaction or about those words over a long session.

**What would reopen it.** A trial showing behaviour the agreement does not reach, such as after a compaction. One hypothesis, seen after unblinding: all four runs with the reference wrote a ledger file, and none of the four without it did, though an excluded pilot without it also did.

**Where to look instead.** In both arms, reports still had arithmetic and interval errors, none named the March 2026 demand change, and most did not flag East's thin data.
